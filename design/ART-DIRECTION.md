# The catchment observatory

An open, materially warm place for public inquiry. The Infosphere Lab belongs to a landscape: sandstone, timber ribs, bronze instruments, weathered green patina, botanical specimens and water. Its geometry borrows the continuity of a frond and the way a tree canopy gathers light, without asserting a biological simulation.

Three native Synth works form its first vocabulary:

- **Canopy of inquiry:** twenty timber ribs rise from limestone terraces and turn gently towards an open oculus. Curved benches leave the centre free for demonstrations and gathering. Keep the roof open; don't fill it with opaque glass or glowing graph nodes.
- **Tidal resonance vessel:** a hollow eighteen-fold fluted surface, a rippled rim and fine bronze rings. Use raking light to reveal the folds. This is authored art, not a claim of measured acoustic resonance.
- **Botanical memory frond:** a tapering spine and twenty curved pinnae. Show structural repetition and scale; label it as authored botanical study, not an anatomically qualified species.

Composition: low foreground stone and water, human-scale instruments at eye level, the canopy's oculus as a framing device, the catchment visible beyond. Light should distinguish smooth bronze, rough stone and warm timber. Avoid neon outlines and uniformly emissive objects. Quiet camera motion is optional and must pause with reduced-motion preference. Explanatory interfaces remain readable and unobtrusive.

These are real loft/revolve outputs exported by Synth as DXF, converted without remodelling to indexed geometry, and sealed as `.10d` through the actual import/compiler path. The browser adapter preserves the exact exported faces. Receipts distinguish native production from browser presentation. Native preview images are geometry reviews, not finished-material artwork approval.

Initial geometry budgets: each asset below 50,000 triangles; no texture downloads required for this art study; all assets are lazy-loadable. These are production constraints, not measured frame-rate guarantees. Target-device qualification belongs to P06/P17; no universal FPS claim is made.
