# Art direction and asset bible — the living world

16 November 2026. Planning specification, not implementation acceptance. AAA is a quality target with falsifiable gates below, not a claim about the current preview. Default language: Australian English (`en-AU`). Canonical work package IDs and ten realm names belong to `MASTER-PLAN.md` and `work-packages.json`; the asset groups here refine those packages rather than introducing a competing programme.

## 1. Experience and native production contract

Make an explorable human-centric computing ecosystem: an inhabited Australian landscape opens into the biosphere, physical Earth, space and infosphere. Information, socioeconomic and knowledge geometry become understandable through relationships, instruments and choices people can use. The personal campsite is a grounded entrance, not the whole world. Natural-world physics, biology, sociology and liberal arts belong to a coherent experience rather than unrelated technology rooms.

Every realm contains **actual underlying data and working capability demonstrations**. Its instruments take real inputs, invoke the real native operation, show the actual output, and expose sources, parameters, uncertainty and receipts. A link or iframe to an old demo cannot satisfy this requirement. The readable, keyboard-operable route uses the same dataset, job and result state; it is not a brochure about the immersive version.

Synth/Vibe and Hypermedia produce native assets; QualiaDB owns retained graphs, geometry and codecs; Etalon supplies applicable render/signal providers; Rust/WASM and QualiaPortal operate and present the world. Generated WASM loading/interoperability glue is permitted. A separately authored JavaScript/Three.js world is not the target. All delivered hero geometry, materials and animation must exist in the actual native pipeline. An image concept, exported file or receipt alone cannot close an asset package.

Preserve legacy demos, sources and immutable benchmark evidence during migration. Recreate their actual inputs, operations, outputs and evidence inside the new environment before marking coverage complete. Historic measurements stay historic, pending tests stay pending, and a successful art slice does not mean the whole site is finished.

## 2. Visual thesis and correction of the rejected preview

Use geological form, ecological structure, weathered craft and precise scientific instruments. Mineral whites, timber, oxidised copper, water, leaf greens and restrained spectral light form the palette. The landscape carries most colour; interfaces and effects earn attention through purpose. Avoid generic cone forests, ellipsoid crowns, neon node galaxies, empty green planes and gratuitous space scenery.

The inspected native preview lost mapped ground detail, bank rocks, layered woodland and atmospheric depth. Hard per-triangle terrain colour bands created jagged river margins; sparse trees replaced a forest; vehicle materials became dark; cameras looked across empty ground and large UI panels obscured subjects. These are specific restoration obligations, documented in the visual parity audit. Concurrent source changes require new captures before any improved appearance is claimed.

The previous Three.js presentation supplies a minimum reference for density, staging and depth, not the final style. It also had generic trees and bare clearings. Finish a continuous campsite–woodland–river slice with credible ground contact, foreground/middle/background, species structure, material scale, soft fill, exposure and a useful route. This is only one of the three mandatory A21 proofs: the other two are an Earth-to-space scientific instrument and an infosphere/commons-to-proof-lab journey. All three require independent review and user review before mass production; success of the campsite slice alone does not unlock broad production. Neither architecture correctness nor triangle counts can pass visual acceptance.

### Four complete journeys

1. **Water becomes knowledge.** Select the river's actual source DEM, read date/resolution, inspect a section and change a bounded study parameter. The native operation creates a new result without overwriting source data. Compare changed elevations and assumptions; open the acquisition record, coordinate transform, operation parameters and result. The readable route performs the same task.
2. **A leaf becomes a produced model.** Select a taxon, follow a leaf-size assertion to its source and see its range. Change an explicit artistic parameter within declared limits. Synth produces the native derivative; front/side/quarter comparison reveals the change. Source-supported geometry and authored choices remain separate.
3. **Sunlight becomes a signal.** Adjust a labelled EM scenario, compile and verify its field, inspect cells and change spectral slices. Visual and supported sonic conversions share the retained field. Any measured solar dataset is a separate dated evidence layer, not retroactive validation of the scenario.
4. **Evidence becomes public conversation.** Select a result, choose language/layout and produce an actual accessible publication. Inspect attribution and purpose conditions, alter a public permission scenario and see the real validator response. Publishing a document does not confer legal authority.

## 4. Human rights and needs demonstrated through use

Public exploration requires no personal identity. Purpose, language, access needs and level of detail are explicit, resettable choices. Do not infer sensitive identity or silently rank visitors. Explain locally retained state and its controls. Observer/status informatics may guide pathways and explanations, but must not fabricate credentials or conceal the factual basis of claims.

Core tasks have keyboard, readable and reduced-motion routes with equivalent data/operations. Audio is optional; meaningful sound has an alternative. A constrained device does not lose knowledge access because it cannot render the hero scene. Every assertion can expose source, uncertainty, conflict and interpretation. Chosen purpose changes explanation, not authority.

Australian English is the default, not the only supported language. Plan BiDi, script/font coverage, longer labels, pluralisation and locale-aware presentation. Country relations and community knowledge need source-specific authority and consent; they are not generic scenery metadata. Source credit, contribution context and legitimate reuse conditions are visible where work is used. No invented impact numbers, testimonials or measurements.

## 8. Falsifiable art subchecks

ART-01–ART-10 refine the master G0–G8 gates; they do not replace or renumber them. G0 controls scope/source admission and G8 requires complete release coverage. The parenthetical mappings below identify where each art subcheck contributes.

**ART-01 — Reference/silhouette (G2):** inspect native front, side and three-quarter views against named references; record corrections. Reject generic stand-ins for specified assets.

**ART-02 — Geometry/surface (G1/G2):** inspect near/far views for invalid faces, intersections, scale, normals, seams and derivative transitions; verify source-face/material correspondence.

**ART-03 — Materials (G1/G2/G3):** neutral and scene-light turntables; prove native UV/tangent/map consumption and expected response. File existence is insufficient.

**ART-04 — Motion/audio (G2/G3/G6):** review complete loops/transitions, muted and reduced-motion modes; validate data-derived sound and retained recording rights.

**ART-05 — Assembled composition (G3):** matched desktop/mobile campsite, river, woodland, commons and observatory captures with UI open/closed. Require layered depth, readable subject, ground contact and useful route. Recover at least the earlier presentation's depth/density before considering the higher target.

**ART-06 — Working exhibit (G4):** execute valid input, invalid input, cancellation and repeat/replay. Match controls to actual results and evidence; complete the same readable task. Old-page links fail this gate.

**ART-07 — Data/rights (G5):** inspect provenance, units, identifiers, uncertainty and sensitivity from the object/result. Reject invented live values and unsupported precision.

**ART-08 — Performance (G7):** agree named device tiers before accepting budgets. Proposed targets for qualification: 60 Hz standard and 30 Hz compact. Report frame-time distribution, p95 stalls, residency, downloads and sustained operation; these are targets, not current achievements. Lower visual tiers preserve data/tasks.

**ART-09 — Audience usefulness (G3/G4/G6):** ordinary visitors can explain what an element does and why it matters; developers run/inspect it; government/UN-related audiences examine agency and provenance; investors distinguish demonstrated from planned capability. Test tasks rather than assuming personas imply understanding.

**ART-10 — Polish/i18n (G6):** test Australian English, long strings, RTL, non-Latin shaping, keyboard/focus/touch, contrast and sensory controls. Externalised strings do not justify claiming all languages complete.

Independent review records accepted, changes required or evidence needed. Repeat affected reviews after material/integration changes. Accepted standalone geometry does not automatically approve placement, lighting or interaction. A23/user review considers concrete native proof experiences, not marketing descriptions.

## 9. Production sequence and swarm handoffs

These sequencing notes follow the master dependency graph; they do not replace it.

**Evidence and language:** A00–A04 qualify the module/data inventory, locality/species, reference boards, camera compositions and shared contracts. Prove one native material-bearing asset and real instrument state contract before large asset batches.

**First of three A21 proofs:** A05–A09/A19 owners work in parallel on surfaces, terrain, woodland, field studio and UI; the integrator assembles a continuous campsite–river route with an actual terrain or botanical operation. Review all applicable master gates and ART subchecks. Fix depth/material/density failures in this proof. In parallel, bounded A21 production must establish the Earth-to-space proof with real data and scale/coordinate handling, and the infosphere/commons-to-proof-lab proof with a real rights-sensitive query and test/benchmark. Each proof includes its dataset browser, working controls, readable equivalent and export. All three require independent and user review before mass production.

**Earth, life, commons:** A08/A11/A13 expand accepted families and data-backed biological/cultural instruments. Operation/data owners work with artists before scenery is complete. Cross-realm tasks preserve Q42 identity.

**Space and fields:** A10/A12 qualify dated sources/computation owners, produce observatory/field instruments, native scale transitions and supported sonification. Scientific framing, sensory controls and device performance are reviewed before spectacle grows.

**Complete ecosystem:** A14–A18/A20–A27 finish Infosphere/Atelier/Proof/Assembly and all site routes, module explanations and native/readable demos. Preserve immutable benchmark evidence and compare actual compatible runs. Full acceptance covers every migration row, not merely the world entrance.

Each package handoff includes brief/dossier, exact operation/API contract, owned paths, input/output resources, Vibe recipes and deterministic receipts where applicable, semantic/material/native manifests, inspected previews, assembled capture requirements, device budget, limitations and reviewer result. Shared integration is serialised; source-owner edits are coordinated. No package is complete while its real operation or readable equivalent is pending.

Before implementation lock the public locality, source releases, representative species, device tiers/residency, compatible material binding route, stable Q42 IDs/job schemas, first user tasks and review cameras. These are authorised team implementation decisions; request user choice only for genuinely unresolved preferences or concrete visual review.

The website repository owns experience content, manifests, recipes, localised presentation and deployment policy. Existing module repositories retain core algorithms/rendering/codecs. The proposed `webizen-website` repository does not change these ownership boundaries. Keep staging/migration paths explicit until the final build and publication path is verified.
