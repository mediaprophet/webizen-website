# Complete capability and demonstration coverage

Planning audit: 16 November 2026. Default language: Australian English (`en-AU`). This is a planning deliverable; it does not certify the proposed exhibits as implemented or rerun any benchmarks. The companion [machine-readable coverage ledger](CAPABILITY-AND-DEMO-COVERAGE.json) is the authoritative per-route assignment and source snapshot.

## Required outcome

The new environment must contain the underlying content, data, fixtures and real capabilities of the existing site. A visitor must be able to operate the instruments, inspect their inputs and outputs, query the data locally where applicable, export the work and understand its evidence **inside the new experience**. Links to old webpages, iframe wrappers, static screenshots and inventory cards do not complete a capability migration. Source links remain useful references after a substantive migration.

The creative objective is a coherent journey across space, Earth, living systems, human communities and the infosphere. Each place must make a real capability understandable through an action: change an observer frame and inspect actual coordinates; alter a watershed input and inspect the real model output; withdraw a permission and observe an actual refusal; edit a rule and inspect the derivation. Beautiful assets and motion support these actions. They cannot replace them.

The present seven-place world is a first scene, not the extent of the product. The 32 DEM subcontracts below, owned by the canonical 28 A00-A27 master packages, organise the complete source inventory without limiting the world to 32 rooms: each original control, sample, dataset, solver case and distinct source variant becomes a subordinate exhibit or document. Shared assets, navigation and execution services may be reused; unique behaviour and evidence must remain discoverable.

Do not promote their prose, a backend selector or a screenshot into a successful execution claim.

### Duplicate and catalogue handling

Two exact-byte duplicate pairs were found: historical `ADP/index.html` with `qdp-dns/index.html`, and their two `old_index.html` files. Their bytes can be stored once, but both historical identifiers, contextual labels and provenance edges remain in the catalogue. These are the only hash-proven duplicates asserted by this audit.

The 63 anatomy previews form 61 structure groups; the heart has distinct entry/view/master paths. Those variants are not discarded because they share an organ name. Record each asset/source/receipt and decide whether it is an alternate view, revision or different model only after byte and visual inspection. Science pages with similar headings, multiple Vibe entry points, graph examples and benchmark hubs are *candidate* semantic overlaps, not approved duplicates. Merge presentation only after action, input, output, provenance and error-case parity is demonstrated.

The complete route ledger below accounts for catalogues, archives, templates and explanations separately. A catalogue page is complete only when all its referenced content is available in the new environment; its links alone are not coverage.

## A common exhibit contract

Each implementation package must deliver a data-backed record with:

- Stable exhibit/case identifiers, realm, source route(s), module owner, source and runtime versions, input hashes, rights/provenance and execution classification.
- Actual datasets, fixtures, inline examples and external dependencies, including a complete extraction manifest. Preserve original bytes privately where appropriate and identify any transformed/public subset. No personal timeline coordinates, exact timestamps or inferred HOME/WORK labels enter the public scene.
- Typed native inputs, bounds, units, seeds, initial state, cancellation and authority requirements. Every visible control binds to a real parameter or action.
- Native-produced assets and native execution/renderer adapters. Browser glue must not author the world. Existing JavaScript reference algorithms are retained only as development/test oracles outside the production application. Production completion requires genuine Rust/Vibe orchestration and actual native or WASM engine execution; generated WASM binding glue is not a JavaScript-authored world. No reference algorithm is silently renamed native.
- Native outputs plus an embedded readable, keyboard-operable equivalent derived from the **same result record**. Tables, charts, explanations, audio descriptions and source editors must remain usable without spatial navigation, motion, colour discrimination or audio.
- Local inspection/query/export of relevant inputs and outputs, with bounded paging for large corpora. Original evidence and new runs remain separate. No old-page iframe or link-out fulfils these requirements.
- An actual execution receipt: runtime/profile, source/input/output identity, correctness result, timing basis where relevant, limitations and exact completion/failure/cancellation state.

Native overlays and accessible HTML projections may have different layouts, but not different data or authority. Internationalisation applies to content, controls, search, units, numerals, text direction, glyph shaping, fonts, subtitles, descriptions and error messages. `en-AU` is the reviewed source locale; other languages require translation provenance and review, rather than a claim that all translations already exist. Observer paths use explicit user choices and scoped public context; no sensitive inference changes a person's rights.

## Native Lab migration without losing evidence

The recently qualified Lab remains an interim instrument interface. Its actual test, graph benchmark, query and historical-record adapters are useful preserved contracts, but the HTML Lab and its source links are **not** completion of this overhaul. Its static native hero is likewise not the final native assurance laboratory.

1. Freeze source identities and current result schemas. Preserve `TestRunner`, suite registration, original assertion bodies, `loadWasm`, runner mode selection, fixture setup/teardown and error/skip semantics. The captured 44-suite registry includes logic, WASM, native and comparison modes; a suite count is not a pass count.
2. Separate execution services from presentation. Introduce typed events for case start/pass/fail/skip/error/cancel, progress, logs and results. A Rust/Vibe-native UI and its embedded accessible projection consume the same events; the production execution/controller layer is also native. The current real-worker cancellation contract must remain actual termination, not a cosmetic progress reset.
3. Preserve each existing engine profile. The original JavaScript logic profile remains an external development/test oracle, outside the production application. Production assertions require a genuine native Rust/Vibe-controlled implementation with exact case/fixture parity. Browser WASM, native daemon and real comparison engines retain their identities and contracts; competitor engines may run through an explicitly identified service/runner when required. Native UI orchestration and actual engine execution are mandatory before completion. Never erase an unsupported profile or replace it with mock output.
4. Recreate the entire benchmark hub, not just the currently integrated 1K/10K graph workload. Include graph, scientific, spatial, geometry/parallel geometry, renderer/resource, zero-heap, Vibe compile/run/purpose clocks, browser LLM and native/comparison workloads. Keep original dataset selection, parameters, query text, expected-result checks, result histories and raw exports.
5. Keep measurement honest. The current graph adapter uses the actual `execute_ntriples_query`, original dataset loader, correctness before timing, ten warm-up iterations and thirty measured samples. Preserve raw samples, mean/p50/p95, units, engine/runtime version, hardware/backend, dataset/query/loader hashes, cache/thermal limitations and collection time. That policy must not overwrite a historical benchmark's different methodology.
6. Preserve the canonical identifier regression: a full 64-bit hash loaded where canonical 60-bit identifiers were expected could disagree with textual query compilation. Keep the original failing fixture, canonical identifier plus recomputed parity fix, and both measurement/input identities. Do not avoid the mismatch by silently substituting numeric queries or changing the measured semantics.
7. The native world pauses or stops nonessential rendering/animation while measuring, and the receipt states whether graphics work was suppressed. Do not merge CPU reference timings, WASM timings, native daemon timings, modelled roofline values and external published comparison numbers into one performance claim.
8. Native service suites run against disposable, explicit fixture environments. A public website must not automatically mutate a visitor's real daemon data, start torrent participation, load private models or exercise personal accounts. This is preservation of genuine execution with appropriate scope, not a reason to omit the cases.
9. Acceptance requires original/new case enumeration equality, preserved fixture hashes, result/error parity, real cancellation, local inspection/export and accessible control parity. The original UI can be retired only after these gates pass; linking it from the new site does not pass them.

## Work packaging and acceptance sequence

Use the DEM identifiers below as independently reviewable swarm work packages, with source owners proposed from the existing module registry. A runtime specialist owns each real execution adapter; a domain specialist owns data and scientific/semantic correctness; an artist owns the native scene and media; an accessibility/localisation specialist owns the equivalent usable interface; an independent verifier checks parity and failure cases. Coordination must use shared typed records, not separate hard-coded versions of a demo.

**Gate 1 — extraction:** enumerate every source action, dataset, fixture, parameter, output, failure, document and asset variant. Review dynamic dependencies. Do not mark extraction complete merely because the HTML route is inventoried.

**Gate 2 — real vertical slice:** one complete representative interaction per execution class, with real bytes and engine, accessible equivalent, inspection/export and receipt. Validate the native rendering/material/audio/input path before multiplying rooms.

**Gate 3 — exhaustive parity:** migrate every remaining subordinate case; retain full archives and catalogues. Compare original and new results, with declared tolerances where exact equality is inappropriate. All 204 route rows and all 44 suites remain individually accountable.

**Gate 4 — integrated artistic review:** desktop/mobile/keyboard/reduced-motion and supported device classes; no washed-out materials, illegible data, clipping, broken scale transitions or controls obscuring the work. Scope fidelity/LOD choices explicitly; preserve source assets and verify native resident geometry/material/texture counts.

**Gate 5 — release evidence:** source/data hashes, qualified runtime versions, accessibility/localisation checks, performance budgets and complete preservation matrix. Archive a rejected/unsupported case with its reason instead of inventing success. Publishing is a separate authorised action, outside this planning task.

### Most underserved capabilities

The present scene and Lab cover only a small part of the potential experience. Highest gaps are: actual cosmic/OCS/spatial interactions; scientific and domain-solver breadth; all anatomy structures and profiles; rights/agreements that change real outcomes; QDNF state/lease/streaming demonstrations; typed media and EMF/QAC/version integrity; GPU/Forge/device certification; real model inference and comparison conditions; and the full benchmark/assurance catalogue. Natural-world depth also requires real ecological datasets and species/landscape distinctions, not more generic trees. See the ecological data-source plan for acquisition and licensing work.

The developer source registry, renderer, production engine, host boundary, application layer and domain repositories each need a visible role in these exhibits. Existing ownership records include unresolved Poet application ownership; reconcile that explicitly. A module card, repository count or high operation count is supporting navigation, never a substitute for a demonstrated capability and its human significance.
