About this journey

A landscape reading

These seven landscape chapters draw on longer visits in a locally held timeline, from mid-November 2025 through mid-November 2026. The available record ends in October 2026. Nearby visits are grouped into broad artistic regions; their names are approximate, not mapped boundaries. Month bands show when a chapter appears, and the reading order follows its first appearance. A visit record is not a campsite. The field studio is a shared artistic setting for exploring these landscapes.

The colours and landscape studies are artistic interpretations. The chapters carry broad regions and months; the original timeline stays local.
